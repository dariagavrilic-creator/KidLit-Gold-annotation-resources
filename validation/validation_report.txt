2. Bias check: does agreement depend on which annotator was favoured?
---------------------------------------------------------------------
   decisions favouring A: 72/93 =  77.4%  [95% CI 67.9–84.7]
   decisions favouring B: 35/45 =  77.8%  [95% CI 63.7–87.5]

   Fisher exact test: p = 1.000, OR = 0.98
   Interpretation: p > 0.05 means the external expert agrees with decisions
   favouring A and B at statistically indistinguishable rates, i.e. no evidence
   that the adjudicator systematically favoured one annotator.
